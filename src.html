Our story

Have you eaten?

Jasmine strung at the doorway. Batter rising overnight. A tumbler of kaapi poured from a height.

Mylapore is the old heart of Chennai, a neighbourhood of temple tanks, flower sellers and kolams drawn fresh every dawn. Its name means the town of the peacock, and its kitchens are known for some of the gentlest vegetarian cooking in South India.

We have carried that table to King Street East. Idli steamed soft, sambar that tastes of the pot, chutneys ground the same morning and dosas crisp off the tawa.
